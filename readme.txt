ArtPad - a shared live canvas for your guild or group.
Works on World of Warcraft: Midnight (12.x) and WoW Forever.

Open it with /ap, the minimap addon menu (addon compartment), or a key
binding (Key Bindings > AddOns > ArtPad).

Drawing
- Left mouse: draw with the selected tool (Pen, Line, Box, Circle, Arrow)
- Right mouse: erase whole strokes under the cursor
- Text: type, press Enter, then click where it should go
- Ctrl + mouse wheel: brush size (also sets text size)
- Mouse wheel: zoom, Shift-drag: move the canvas
- Ctrl+Z: undo your last stroke
- Esc: close

Everyone using ArtPad in your guild (or party/raid in group mode) sees your
strokes live as you draw them. People who log in later receive the current
canvas from someone online, and the canvas is saved between sessions.

Blizzard blocks addon communication during raid encounters, Mythic+ and
rated PvP. ArtPad closes in combat and catches up afterwards.

Commands
/ap                      toggle the canvas
/ap guild | group        who you share with
/ap clear | undo
/ap sync                 ask others for the latest canvas
/ap adminonly            (group mode) only show drawings from leader/assists
/ap notify               chat hint when someone draws while you're not looking
/ap persist              keep the canvas between sessions
/ap minimap              toggle the minimap button (needs LibDBIcon from another addon)
/ap debug | selftest
